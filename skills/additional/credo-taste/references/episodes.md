# Episodes

Reasoning episodes from Carlini's [How to win a best paper award](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2026/how-to-win-a-best-paper-award.html), paraphrased for use as analogies. Each gives the situation, the move he made, and when a user's situation resembles it. Say the resemblance, then ask what the analog is; do not present the episode as a rule.

| # | Situation | The move | Use as analogy when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | The membership-inference literature made no sense to him: attacks that trained networks on model outputs, odd metrics. | He set the field aside, built his own attack after one paper gave him a workable idea, then read everything so his correction would land. The paper reframed the problem as a hypothesis test; the field followed. | The user finds a literature confused or wrong and is tempted either to follow it or to skip it. The move is: do your version first, then read all of it. |
| E2 | Poisoning semi-supervised learning in 2020, before training on large unlabeled data was standard. | The introduction argued where machine learning was heading before arguing the attack. Rejected several times because reviewers did not believe the setting mattered; accepted on the fourth try with an award. | The user is early. Reviewers reject the premise, not the work. The move is: sell the future in the introduction, then persist. |
| E3 | Studying LAION-5B after two poisoning papers, not looking for an attack. | He noticed that images were referenced by URL and that expired domains could be bought, which made poisoning web-scale data practical for under a hundred dollars. | A new artifact appears and the user asks whether there is anything there. The move is: check the fixed list of failure modes and look for the practical variant of a known one. The pass and its list live in praxis-direction. |
| E4 | Adi Shamir gave a talk extending Carlini's 2020 model-stealing paper. | Carlini asked whether one direction was possible, spent the next twelve hours on a partial solution, and emailed it. That became the EuroCrypt 2025 best paper. | The user wants to collaborate with someone. The move is: bring a partial solution to a problem that person already cares about. |
| E5 | In 2017 Anish Athalye asked him to break one ICLR paper. | Breaking it revealed a general weakness, so they broke the other defenses with the same idea; that became the ICML 2018 best paper on obfuscated gradients. | A small request or side task reveals a general tool. The move is: notice generality and widen the target. |
| E6 | GPT-2 came out while he was mid-way through other papers. | He dropped the ongoing work, which he no longer remembers, and started studying language models. In hindsight he should have pivoted harder and earlier. | Something clearly more important arrives. The move is: check the pull of the new, then pivot without sunk cost if it survives that check. |
| E7 | Submitting a model-stealing paper to Crypto 2020, a theory venue that rarely takes machine learning. | Three pages of introduction easing theoretical cryptographers from their world into his; those pages mattered more than the results. | The reader is far from the user's world. The move is: spend the introduction on the journey, not the contribution. |
| E8 | The real message of "On Evaluating Adversarial Robustness" was that almost everyone evaluates wrong and the reader will too. | Saying it outright would have drawn "not me". The paper laid out evidence so the reader arrived at the conclusion themselves. | The user's claim will be dismissed if stated. The move is: evidence first, conclusion last, and let the reader say it. |
| E9 | A result held "sometimes". | Several extra hours of experiments to be able to write "usually". | The user has a hedged sentence because the evidence is thin. The move is: run the experiment that removes the hedge, or keep the hedge honestly. |
| E10 | He is the world expert at breaking image adversarial-example defenses, and that skill no longer wins awards. | He states that impact is skill multiplied by the importance of the area right now, keeps the work as a hobby, and moves his main effort to new ground. | The user is strongest in an area the field has moved past. The move is: name it, and put the main effort where skill and current importance meet. |

Two background facts from the essay to keep in view. He starts about five projects for every one he finishes. Half of his papers came from a spontaneous conversation or from happening to read a paper with a usable tool while thinking about a problem; being around and sharing ideas is part of the method, not luck alone.
